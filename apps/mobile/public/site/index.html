Questions

Is it a real Linux?

It is a faithful simulation running entirely on your device: a virtual file system with permissions, a bash-like shell with pipes, redirects, loops and scripts, and about 120 commands whose output matches GNU coreutils. Nothing you type can harm your phone.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything works offline and there is no sign-up. Your progress stays on your device.

Can I continue on a computer?

Settings → Transfer progress: “Save / send file” or “Copy as text”, then “Open file” (or paste the text) in the browser version on your computer and choose “Merge” or “Replace”.

Which languages are supported?

English, Ukrainian, Russian and Polish — everything is translated, including “Explain a command”.

Is it really free?

Yes — every course, mission and the exam. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

Does the app collect data or show ads?

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no network requests. See the privacy policy.
