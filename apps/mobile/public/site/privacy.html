Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers both apps — Linux Terminal Trainer (free, com.vadymzelenko.linuxtrainer) and Linux Terminal Trainer Pro (paid, com.vadymzelenko.linuxtrainer.pro) — and the browser version.

In short

The apps do not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. They make no network requests. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

  • Your learning progress, settings and sandbox machines are stored only on your device. Deleting the app or using “Reset all progress” removes them.
  • Android’s own device backup may include this data if you have backups turned on in your phone settings.

Transfer progress

Settings → Transfer progress creates a backup file or text only when you ask for it. You choose where it goes in the system share sheet (for example your own e-mail, a messenger or a cloud drive). The developer never receives it. The backup contains your progress, settings, sandbox machines and the name you typed for the certificate, if any.

Permissions and device features

  • Daily reminders are local notifications, enabled only after you turn them on.
  • Sounds and vibration are generated on the device. The apps do not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.
  • The advertising ID is not used.
  • “GitHub” inside the lessons is simulated on your device; the apps never connect to the real github.com.

Purchases

Linux Terminal Trainer Pro is bought once through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google under Google’s privacy policy; the developer receives no payment details.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the Google Play Data safety forms will be updated before the new version is released.
