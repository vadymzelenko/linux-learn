Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Linux Terminal Trainer app (com.vadymzelenko.linuxtrainer) published on Google Play by VZstudio, and its browser version. It explains what data the app handles and why.

In short

The app does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. It has no account or sign-in and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

  • Your learning progress (lessons, tasks, missions, scores, review schedule), settings and sandbox machines are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage.
  • Commands you type are run by a simulator inside the app. They are never executed on a real system or uploaded anywhere.
  • Android’s own device backup may include this data if you have backups turned on in your phone settings; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by the app.

Transfer progress

Settings → Transfer progress creates a backup file or text only when you ask for it. You choose where it goes in the system share sheet (for example your own e-mail, a messenger or a cloud drive). The developer never receives it. The backup contains your progress, settings, sandbox machines and the name you typed for the certificate, if any.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: daily reminders are local notifications, requested only after you turn them on in Settings. No push service is used.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings.
  • Sounds are generated on the device. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or your files.
  • The advertising ID is not used.
  • “GitHub” inside the lessons is simulated on your device; the app never connects to the real github.com.
  • The only time the app opens the internet is when you tap a link such as “Privacy policy” or “Help”. The page opens in your browser.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data, and we do not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. The app is distributed through Google Play: Google may process install data and, if you allow it on your device, crash and performance statistics, under the Google Privacy Policy. This website and the support page are hosted on GitHub; when you open them, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays on your device until you delete it: Settings → Reset all progress, “Clear storage” for the app in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. We keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing else to delete and no account to close.

Security

App data lives in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Nothing is sent over the network, so there is nothing to intercept in transit.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the app does not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you; all app data is under your control on your device.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is a general educational app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if optional ads or crash reports are added), this page and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the new version is released, and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/vadymzelenko/linux-learn/issues or write to the developer e-mail shown on the app’s Google Play page.
