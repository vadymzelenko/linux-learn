Support

Found a bug, a wrong answer or a typo in a lesson? Write to support@example.com — tell which app (Free or Pro), the lesson or command, and what you expected. A screenshot helps a lot.

Move my progress to Pro or to another device

  1. In the app you are moving from: Settings → Transfer progress → Save / send file (or Copy as text).
  2. In the other app or in the browser version: Settings → Transfer progress → Open file (or paste the text and tap Import text).
  3. Choose Merge to keep the best of both, or Replace to overwrite.

I bought Pro on another phone

Open Google Play with the same Google account and install Linux Terminal Trainer Pro again — purchases are tied to your account. Then transfer your progress as above.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play: request a refund.

My command is correct but the task is not accepted

Tasks are checked by the result (files, output, state), not by the exact text. Open the hint, compare the result with the goal, and if you still think it is right, send us the command — we will fix the check.

Reset everything

Settings → Reset all progress. Sandbox machines can be reset one by one from the Sandbox tab.
