# Privacy Policy — Linux Terminal Trainer

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

This policy covers the **Linux Terminal Trainer** app by VZstudio (`com.vadymzelenko.linuxtrainer`) and its browser version. The app is free and has no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

- The app does **not collect, store on servers or share any personal data**. They make no network requests.
- Your progress, settings and sandbox machines are stored **only on your device**. Deleting the app or using “Reset all progress” removes them. Android’s own device backup may include this data if you have backups turned on in your phone settings.
- **Transfer progress** (Settings → Transfer progress) creates a backup file or text only when you ask for it. Where it goes is up to you: you pick the destination in the system share sheet (for example your own e-mail, a messenger or a cloud drive). The developer never receives it. The backup contains your learning progress, settings, sandbox machines and the name you typed for the certificate, if any.
- Daily reminders are local notifications, enabled only after you turn them on.
- No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking SDKs; the advertising ID is not used.
- Sounds and vibration are generated on the device; the app does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.
- “GitHub” inside the lessons is simulated on your device; the app never connect to the real github.com.
- The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this ever changes, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the new version is released.
