# Privacy Policy — Linux Terminal Trainer

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Linux Terminal Trainer is a free app. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

- The app does **not collect, store on servers or share any personal data**. It makes no network requests.
- Your progress, settings and sandbox machines are stored **only on your device** (SQLite). Deleting the app or using “Reset all progress” removes them. Android’s own device backup may include this data if you have backups turned on in your phone settings.
- **Transfer progress** (Settings → Transfer progress) creates a backup file or text only when you ask for it. Where it goes is up to you: you pick the destination in the system share sheet (for example your own e-mail, a messenger or a cloud drive). The developer never receives it. The backup contains your learning progress, settings and sandbox machines, and the name you typed for the certificate, if any.
- Daily reminders are local notifications, enabled only after you turn them on.
- The app contains **no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking SDKs**, and does not use the advertising ID.
- Sounds and vibration are generated on the device; the app does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.
- “GitHub” inside the lessons is simulated on your device; the app never connects to the real github.com.

If this ever changes, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the new version is released.
