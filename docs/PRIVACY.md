# Privacy policy — Linux Terminal Trainer

The policy is published at **https://vadymzelenko.github.io/linux-learn/privacy/** (GitHub Pages, workflow *Website*) and, if the web version is deployed to Vercel, at `/privacy` there.

Source of truth: [`apps/mobile/public/site/privacy.html`](../apps/mobile/public/site/privacy.html) — edit it there; both hosts publish that file.

Summary: no account, no personal data collected or shared, no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking; progress stays on the device; reminders are local notifications; backups are created and shared only by the user. Contact: https://github.com/vadymzelenko/linux-learn/issues or the e-mail on the Google Play page.
