# Privacy Policy — Linux Terminal Trainer

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

- The app has **no accounts** and does not ask for personal data.
- Your progress, settings and sandbox machines are stored **only on your device** (SQLite). Deleting the app or using “Reset all progress” removes them.
- Daily reminders are local notifications, enabled only after you turn them on.
- The app currently contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs** and sends nothing over the network. If ads are added in a future version, this policy and the Play Store Data Safety form will be updated first, and users in the EEA/UK will see a consent form before any ad is loaded.
- Sounds and vibration are generated on the device; the app does not use the microphone or any other sensor.
- Crash reports, if added, are sent only with your consent.
